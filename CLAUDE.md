## How to work with Ulysse

- Plan first. For anything touching more than one file, write the plan in plain English and wait for a yes.
- After each change, explain in plain English what each part does, so he can repeat it to a judge.
- If a fix fails twice, stop. State your best diagnosis of the cause and ask. Do not keep guessing.
- Ask before adding a dependency.
- If the ticket and AGENTS.md disagree, follow AGENTS.md and say so.
